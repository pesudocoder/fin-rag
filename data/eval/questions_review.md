## Design constraints

- **single_company_scope** - Every answerable question targets ONE company. Phase 5 measured mean unique companies in the top-5 at 1.625, and cross-company probes collapsed onto a single filer (the FX probe returned 5 Coca-Cola chunks). A comparative question would fail at retrieval, so it would measure a retrieval limitation rather than grounding.
- **no_table_figures_in_answerable** - Phase 4 destroys table structure during HTML extraction, so figures reach the model stripped of row and period labels. Figure questions are therefore legitimately unanswerable and are placed in the adversarial category, not the answerable one.
- **no_reuse** - No question reuses a Phase 5 retrieval probe or a Phase 6 smoke-test question. Those informed the design and tuning of the system; evaluating on them would be testing against data the system was fitted to.
- **phrasing_variation** - Roughly half the answerable questions use the filings' own vocabulary and half are paraphrased into plain language, so retrieval is not only tested on keyword overlap.

## Retrieval reference points (Phase 5, this corpus)

- random chunk-pair mean cosine (noise floor): **0.2819**
- highest out-of-corpus probe score: **0.463**
- lowest legitimate top-1 score: **0.575**

Scores between 0.463 and 0.575 are the **ambiguous band** - the range where no score threshold can separate answerable from unanswerable. Questions landing there are the ones that make semantic refusal necessary.
